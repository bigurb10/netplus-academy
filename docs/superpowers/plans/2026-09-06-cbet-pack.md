# CBET Course Pack Plan (BiomedTech Academy)

**Goal:** A complete `courses/cbet/` pack in the FieldReady Academy format for the AAMI/ACI Certified Biomedical Equipment Technician exam: manifest, cheat sheet, 43 original lessons across 9 units with a deeper explanation each, five or more original questions per lesson (about 215), generators for computable topics, course page, and catalog card.

**Status:** Plan written 2026-09-06. Exam outline verified against two independent published summaries (the AAMI candidate handbook itself was not fetchable); verify domain weights against the current handbook before launch.

## Exam facts (verified 2026-09-06)

- 165 multiple-choice questions, 150 scored and 15 unscored pretest items, 3 hours, closed book.
- Passing: criterion-referenced cut score; published guidance puts it near 116 of 165, about 70%. The course bar is 85%.
- Domains and weights: Healthcare Technology Function and Operation 30%; Healthcare Technology Problem Solving 30%; Healthcare Information Technology 17%; Public Safety in the Healthcare Facility 10%; Anatomy and Physiology 7%; Fundamentals of Electricity and Electronics 6%.

## Naming

Course name **BiomedTech Academy**, badge **BT**, id `cbet`. "CBET" is the certification's name and is used only to describe the exam the course prepares for, never as the course's own brand.

## Content rules

Original prose and original questions. No reference to any book, author, or page. The Mometrix guide in `E:\CERT GUIDES\CBET` is for ideas and for checking numeric facts only. Every lesson gets a "Need a deeper explanation?" walkthrough with an analogy, a diagram or table, worked examples, "how the exam asks it", and "what to memorize". Numeric safety limits must be stated with the standard they come from (IEC 60601-1 or NFPA 99) because editions differ.

## Units and lessons (43)

Domain in brackets. Course order teaches fundamentals first, then devices, then problem solving, then healthcare IT.

### Unit 1: Anatomy and Physiology for the Technician [1]
- u1l1 Body Organization, Directional Terms, and Medical Terminology. Cells, tissues, organs, systems; anterior/posterior, superior/inferior, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, supine/prone; common prefixes, roots, and suffixes; vital sign normal ranges.
- u1l2 The Cardiovascular System. Chambers, valves, circulation paths, conduction system (SA, AV, His, branches, Purkinje), cardiac cycle, blood pressure and MAP, what ECG and BP devices actually measure.
- u1l3 The Respiratory System. Airway, lungs, gas exchange, ventilation versus oxygenation, lung volumes, what oximetry, capnography, and ventilators measure or replace.
- u1l4 The Nervous and Musculoskeletal Systems. Brain regions, spinal cord, peripheral nerves, EEG and EMG basis, muscles, bones and joints, nerve stimulation and why it matters for electrical safety and ESU frequency.
- u1l5 Digestive, Renal, Endocrine, and Other Systems. GI tract and enteral feeding, kidneys and the basis of dialysis, endocrine basics and glucose, blood composition, the immune system and infection.

### Unit 2: Fundamentals of Electricity and Electronics [3]
- u2l1 Voltage, Current, Resistance, and Power. Ohm's law, power formulas, series and parallel circuits, Kirchhoff's rules in plain terms, worked circuit problems.
- u2l2 AC, Transformers, and Hospital Power. RMS and peak, frequency, single- and three-phase, transformers and turns ratio, isolation transformers, grounding, isolated power systems and the line isolation monitor, GFCI, wire color codes.
- u2l3 Passive Components, Filters, and Time Constants. Resistors and color code, capacitors, inductors, reactance, RC and RL time constants, low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and notch filters, where each appears in a medical device.
- u2l4 Semiconductors, Power Supplies, and Amplifiers. Diodes and rectifiers, regulators, linear versus switching supplies, transistors, op-amp rules, instrumentation amplifiers and common-mode rejection, why ECG front ends look the way they do.
- u2l5 Digital Basics, Signals, and Test Equipment. Number systems, logic gates, ADC and DAC, sampling and Nyquist, resolution, DMM, oscilloscope, function generator, how to measure safely.

### Unit 3: Public Safety in the Healthcare Facility [2]
- u3l1 Electrical Safety: Shock, Leakage, and Isolation. Physiological effects of current by magnitude, macroshock versus microshock, leakage current sources, grounding, isolated power, LIM, GFCI, protective earth.
- u3l2 Electrical Safety Testing and Standards. IEC 60601-1 classes and applied-part types B, BF, CF, defibrillation-proof; leakage limits by standard; ground resistance; safety analyzer tests and sequence; NFPA 99 and NFPA 70 roles.
- u3l3 Infection Control and Personal Safety. Standard precautions, hand hygiene, PPE donning and doffing, bloodborne pathogens, sharps, cleaning versus disinfection versus sterilization, Spaulding classification, device cleaning before service.
- u3l4 Fire, Medical Gas, Radiation, Laser, and MRI Safety. Fire triangle, PASS and RACE, extinguisher classes, oxygen-enriched atmospheres, cylinder colors and pin index, MRI zones and projectile hazards, ionizing radiation and ALARA, laser classes and eye protection.
- u3l5 Regulations, Incident Reporting, and Recalls. FDA medical device reporting timelines, recall classes, the Joint Commission and CMS, OSHA and hazard communication, sequestering equipment after an incident, root cause analysis basics.

### Unit 4: Physiological Monitoring [4]
- u4l1 ECG: Electrodes, Leads, Waveform, and Paper Math. Einthoven's triangle and law, limb and augmented leads, precordial placement, 12 leads from 10 electrodes, P-QRS-T, intervals, 25 mm/s, rate from R-R, monitoring versus diagnostic bandwidth, artifacts and their causes.
- u4l2 Blood Pressure: NIBP and Invasive Monitoring. Oscillometric method and MAP, cuff sizing rules and errors, invasive transducers, zeroing and leveling at the phlebostatic axis, damping and the fast-flush test, transducer sensitivity.
- u4l3 Pulse Oximetry and Capnography. Two wavelengths and the ratio, normal values, sources of error, mainstream versus sidestream EtCO2, the capnogram phases, what each device cannot tell you.
- u4l4 Temperature, Respiration, Multiparameter Monitors, and Telemetry. Thermistors and thermocouples, impedance pneumography, central stations, alarm priorities and colors, telemetry bands, battery and antenna issues.
- u4l5 Fetal Monitors, EEG, EMG, and Sleep Studies. Doppler fetal heart rate and tocodynamometry, normal fetal rates, EEG frequency bands and the 10-20 system, EMG and nerve conduction basics.

### Unit 5: Therapeutic and Life-Support Devices [4]
- u5l1 Defibrillators, AEDs, and External Pacemakers. Monophasic versus biphasic, energies, synchronized cardioversion, AED logic, energy storage math, testing with a defibrillator analyzer, pacing modes.
- u5l2 Ventilators and Respiratory Therapy Equipment. Modes, volume versus pressure control, PEEP, FiO2, tidal volume and minute volume, alarms and their meanings, oxygen concentrators, CPAP and BiPAP, humidification, oxygen analyzer calibration.
- u5l3 Infusion Pumps and Enteral Pumps. Large-volume, syringe, and PCA pumps; mechanisms; free-flow protection; occlusion and air-in-line detection; drug libraries; accuracy testing; rate and dose math.
- u5l4 Electrosurgery, Lasers, and Surgical Equipment. Monopolar versus bipolar, cut and coag waveforms, frequency and why it is safe, return electrode monitoring and burns, ESU analyzer tests, lasers, OR tables, lights, and surgical microscopes.
- u5l5 Dialysis, Anesthesia, and Neonatal Equipment. Hemodialysis components, conductivity and temperature limits, blood leak and air detectors, water treatment; anesthesia machine gas supply, vaporizers, hypoxic guard, scavenging; incubators, warmers, and phototherapy.

### Unit 6: Imaging, Laboratory, and General Equipment [4]
- u6l1 Diagnostic Imaging: X-ray, Fluoroscopy, and CT. Tube, kVp, mA, mAs, generators, detectors, collimation, AEC, inverse square law, CT and Hounsfield units, dose management.
- u6l2 Ultrasound, MRI, and Nuclear Medicine. Piezoelectric transducers, frequency versus penetration, Doppler, MRI magnets, gradients, RF, quench and safety, gamma cameras and PET basics.
- u6l3 Laboratory Analyzers, Sterilizers, and Washers. Centrifuges, spectrophotometry and Beer-Lambert, chemistry and hematology analyzers, steam sterilizer parameters, ethylene oxide and hydrogen peroxide, washer-disinfectors.
- u6l4 Beds, Lifts, Suction, and General Hospital Equipment. Beds and scales, patient lifts, suction regulators, sequential compression, thermometers, endoscopes and video systems, common failures.

### Unit 7: Problem Solving, part 1 [5]
- u7l1 Troubleshooting Methodology for Medical Devices. Confirm the complaint, check the simple things, use error versus device error, isolate by subsystem, half-splitting, safe testing, repair, verification and safety test after repair, documentation.
- u7l2 Power Supplies, Batteries, and Charging Problems. Linear and switching supply failures, ripple, battery chemistries and voltages, capacity and runtime, charging faults, battery management programs.
- u7l3 Signal, Cable, Connector, and Interference Problems. Artifact and noise sources, broken leads and intermittent connectors, EMI and RFI, grounding and ground loops, cable testing, ESD damage.
- u7l4 Mechanical, Pneumatic, and Fluid Problems. Motors and gears, pumps and valves, tubing and leaks, occlusions, calibration drift, sensors that go out of range.
- u7l5 Device-Specific Troubleshooting Scenarios. Common symptom-to-cause tables for monitors, infusion pumps, ventilators, defibrillators, ESUs, and imaging, and the first check for each.

### Unit 8: Problem Solving, part 2 [5]
- u8l1 Preventive Maintenance and Inspection Programs. Scheduled versus risk-based PM, alternative equipment maintenance, inspection procedures, PM after repair, documentation and metrics.
- u8l2 Calibration, Performance Verification, and Test Equipment. Simulators and analyzers by device type, traceability, tolerance, when a device fails verification, safety analyzer use.
- u8l3 Prioritizing Work, Risk, and Recalls. Prioritization by patient risk and criticality, life-support first, incident response and sequestering, recalls and safety alerts, root cause analysis.
- u8l4 Equipment Management: Inventory, Lifecycle, Acquisition, and Documentation. CMMS, asset tagging, incoming inspection, service manuals and parts, end of life and disposition, cost of ownership, service contracts.

### Unit 9: Healthcare Information Technology [6]
- u9l1 Networking Fundamentals for Medical Devices. IP addresses, masks, gateways, DHCP, DNS, VLANs, switches, routers, access points, cabling, bandwidth and latency, static addresses for devices.
- u9l2 Wireless, Telemetry, and Remote Connectivity. Wi-Fi bands and standards, WPA2 and WPA3, WMTS, RTLS, coverage and interference, remote service and VPN.
- u9l3 Interoperability: HL7, DICOM, PACS, and the EHR. HL7 v2 messages and FHIR, DICOM and modality worklists, PACS, EHR and EMR, middleware, time synchronization, IHE profiles in one sentence.
- u9l4 Cybersecurity for Medical Devices. HIPAA and PHI, NIST framework functions, HITRUST, HHS cybersecurity performance goals, MDS2, SBOM, patching and segmentation, access control and encryption, incident handling.
- u9l5 Computers, Operating Systems, and IT Troubleshooting. PC hardware, operating systems, drivers, backups, common troubleshooting with ping, ipconfig, tracert, link lights, and cable checks.

Free set: the first 10 lessons in course order are Units 1 and 2.

## Generators (`courses/cbet/generators.js`)

- `u2l1` Ohm's law and power: given two of V, I, R, or P, find the third; series and parallel resistance.
- `u2l3` RC time constant and 63 percent charge.
- `u2l5` binary, decimal, and hexadecimal conversion.
- `u3l1` current-effect thresholds and leakage limits by applied-part type (state the standard).
- `u4l1` heart rate from R-R spacing in small or large boxes at 25 mm/s.
- `u5l2` minute volume from tidal volume and rate; FiO2 from oxygen analyzer readings.
- `u5l3` infusion rate from volume and time; dose math from mg/kg/hr and concentration.
- `u1l1` unit conversions: kg and lb, C and F, mmHg and cmH2O, mL and L.
- `u9l1` IP addressing: is the device on the same subnet as the gateway.
