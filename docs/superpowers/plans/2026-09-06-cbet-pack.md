# CBET Course Pack Plan (CBET Academy)

**Goal:** A complete `courses/cbet/` pack in the FieldReady Academy format for the AAMI/ACI Certified Biomedical Equipment Technician exam: manifest, cheat sheet, 45 original lessons across 9 units with a deeper explanation each, five or more original questions per lesson (about 225), generators for computable topics, course page, and catalog card.

**Status:** Plan revised 2026-09-06 against the official AAMI outline Blake pasted. Cheat sheet written. Lessons in progress.

## Exam facts (from the official AAMI outline)

- 165 multiple-choice questions, 3 hours, closed book; a simple calculator is allowed. 150 scored plus 15 unscored pretest items.
- Passing: criterion-referenced cut score set by the modified Angoff method; published guidance puts it near 116 of 165 (about 70%). The course bar is 85%.
- Domains: Healthcare Technology and Function 30%; Healthcare Technology Problem Solving 30%; Healthcare Information Technology 17%; Public Safety in the Healthcare Facility 10%; Anatomy and Physiology 7%; Fundamentals of Electricity and Electronics 6%.

## What the outline names (every lesson maps to these)

- **A&P**: circulatory, respiratory, gastrointestinal, nervous, musculoskeletal, endocrine systems; heart, lungs, brain, skin; medical terminology.
- **Electricity and electronics**: Ohm's law, active and passive devices, schematics for troubleshooting; power conditioning, distribution, storage: LIM, transformers, batteries, UPS.
- **Healthcare IT**: HIPAA, NIST framework, HITRUST, HHS cybersecurity performance goals; hardware troubleshooting (power supplies, peripherals, drives, cables, connectors); software update and patch (Windows, Office, Linux, Unix); wired and wireless networks and components (routers, switches, access points and controllers, KVMs, cables, virtual servers, LAN, WAN, cloud) with tools (cable tracers, PING, networking commands); device integration to the EMR directly or through middleware; identifiers (IP, MAC).
- **Function**: physiological concepts (Korotkoff sounds, Einthoven's triangle, QRS, dicrotic notch); test equipment (safety analyzer, defibrillator analyzer, ESU analyzer, simulators, DVM, meters); units (mmHg, PSI, bar, Fahrenheit, cm H2O, lumens, joules); monitoring systems (fetal, telemetry, physiological, vital signs); parameters (EtCO2, ECG, EEG, NIBP, IBP, oximetry, TOCO, FHR, respiration, temperature); diagnostic equipment (otoscope, ophthalmoscope, scales, stress test, audiometer, ECG, spirometer, ultrasound); infusion (feeding pumps, LVP, syringe pumps, PCA, injectors); therapeutic (neonatal and pediatric, patient temperature management, aspiration, SCD, physical therapy); perioperative (ESU, video integration, tourniquets, sterilization, fluid warmer, tables, lights, surgical microscopes); life support (defibrillators, anesthesia machines, ventilators, balloon pumps, external pacemakers); laboratory (centrifuges, incubators, rockers, refrigerators, freezers, microscopes, water baths, analyzers, cryostats, microtomes).
- **Problem solving**: fault conditions of modules and subsystems including power supplies; prioritizing by risk, urgency, and downtime; device error versus use error; educating staff and communicating with clinicians, staff, and manufacturers; localized device problem versus system-wide network problem; fault conditions and corrective action for each equipment category above.
- **Safety**: NFPA 99 and IEC 60601 electrical safety (micro and macro shock, cord color coding, GFCI, limits); safety data sheets and hazard signage (laser, MRI, ionizing radiation, patient precautions); universal precautions, infection control, PPE; CMS and the organizations that accredit on its behalf; ANSI/AAMI EQ56, EQ89, EQ93, EQ103 (AEM); using the CMMS to document, track history, and report.

## Naming

Course name **CBET Academy**, badge **CB**, id `cbet`. Blake approved the name on 2026-09-06.

## Content rules

Original prose and original questions; no reference to any book, author, or page; the Mometrix guide in `E:\CERT GUIDES\CBET` is for ideas and number checks only. Every lesson has a deeper explanation with an analogy, a diagram or table, worked examples, "how the exam asks it", and "what to memorize". Every electrical-safety limit names its standard (IEC 60601-1 or NFPA 99).

## Units and lessons (45)

### Unit 1: Anatomy and Physiology [1]
- u1l1 Body Organization, Terminology, and the Skin
- u1l2 The Cardiovascular System (blood flow path, conduction, cardiac cycle, blood pressure and MAP, Korotkoff sounds, dicrotic notch, common abnormalities)
- u1l3 The Respiratory System (airway, gas exchange, ventilation versus oxygenation, lung volumes, spirometry values, abnormalities)
- u1l4 The Nervous and Musculoskeletal Systems (brain regions, autonomic effects on heart and breathing, action potentials and why current stimulates, EEG and EMG basis, bones, joints, muscle types, abnormalities)
- u1l5 Gastrointestinal, Endocrine, Renal, and Blood (GI tract and feeding, pancreas and glucose, thyroid and adrenals, kidneys and dialysis basis, blood components, abnormalities)

### Unit 2: Fundamentals of Electricity and Electronics [3]
- u2l1 Voltage, Current, Resistance, and Power
- u2l2 AC, Transformers, Hospital Power Distribution, and Isolation (essential electrical system, red outlets, isolated power, LIM, GFCI, cord color coding)
- u2l3 Passive Components, Filters, and Reading Schematics
- u2l4 Active Devices, Power Supplies, and Amplifiers
- u2l5 Batteries, UPS, and Power Conditioning

### Unit 3: Public Safety in the Healthcare Facility [2]
- u3l1 Electrical Safety: Shock, Leakage, Grounding, and GFCI
- u3l2 NFPA 99, IEC 60601-1, Limits, and Safety Testing
- u3l3 Infection Control, Universal Precautions, and PPE
- u3l4 Hazard Communication, Signage, Fire, Gas, Radiation, Laser, and MRI Safety
- u3l5 CMS, Accreditation, AAMI EQ Standards, and the CMMS

### Unit 4: Physiological Monitoring [4]

### Unit 5: Diagnostic, Infusion, Therapeutic, and Laboratory Equipment [4]
- u5l1 Diagnostic Equipment (otoscope, ophthalmoscope, audiometer, spirometer, stress test, scales, ultrasound)
