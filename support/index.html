Support

Help with the courses, your account, and the ServiceForge connector.

Talk to us

The fastest way to reach us is the FieldReady Academy Discord. Post in the support channel and a real person answers; other technicians often answer first.

Join the Discord

Requests about your data (a copy, a correction, or deletion of your account) can be made there as a direct message to a moderator, or by email to support@fieldreadyacademy.com.

ServiceForge connector

  • Is it up? The status endpoint should return {"status":"ok"}.
  • The assistant cannot see the tools. Remove the connector and add it again; the address must end in /mcp.
  • The sign-in page never opens. In the connector's settings, set the OAuth client to register automatically and try again.
  • A model is missing. Say so; it is added the first time you log something against it.
  • A log was a mistake. Tell your assistant to retract it; only the technician who wrote it can.
  • Setup steps for each assistant are on the ServiceForge page.

Courses and account

  • Progress is saved in your browser and, when you are signed in, to your account so it follows you between devices. Signing in on a second device merges the two.
  • Sign-in is by email and a one-time code; there is no password to reset.
  • Found an error in a lesson or question? Use the feedback button on it; we read every one.
